About

It started with a sentence.

DIRBALK is a Jordanian brand built around real life — the people you leave the house for, and the ones who tell you to take care on your way out.

We make a few pieces, slowly: raw denim, heavy cotton, quiet details. Clothes that look like nothing much from across the street, and like a lot up close.

Our approach

Three rules we keep.

01

Nothing loud.

No logo across the chest. No text shouting at strangers. Whoever's wearing it isn't an ad — not for us, not for anyone.

02

Quiet details.

Amber stitching, matte silver hardware, a clean surface. Nothing shouts — everything is considered.

03

Made to stay.

13oz raw denim that changes with you and 260gsm cotton that holds its shape. Pieces you keep, not pieces you replace.
