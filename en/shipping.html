Shipping & Returns

What you need to know before you order.

Payment

Cash on delivery. You pay when the piece arrives and you've seen it.

Online payments are coming soon.

Delivery

Delivery within Jordan takes 2–4 business days from order confirmation.

After you order, we'll confirm your address on WhatsApp before shipping — make sure your number's right.

We'll confirm delivery fees with you based on your area.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Exchanges are possible within 7 days of delivery, as long as the piece is in its original condition — not worn outside the house, not washed, with all its details intact.

Every piece has its own number. An exchange means a different piece — a different number — in your size.

To avoid this altogether, check the size guide on each product page before you order — measurements in centimeters, with an easy way to compare against something you already own.

Returns

If your piece arrives with a manufacturing defect — rare, but it happens — send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We'll sort it out directly: exchange or full refund.

Questions?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us — have your order number (DB-XXXX) ready.
