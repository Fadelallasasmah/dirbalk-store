Prices are in Jordanian Dinars (JD). Payment is cash on delivery. Delivery fees depend on your area and are confirmed with you before shipping.

We deliver within Jordan, usually in 2–4 business days from confirmation. Please make sure someone can receive the order at the address you give us.
